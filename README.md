A letter in one image convincing the AI judge to visit your house: a warm "Comfort & Cookies" evening invitation — grandma's oatmeal-raisin cookies with frosting piped like tiny circuits, the comfiest chair in the house (real springs, zero algorithms), and a garden window that blooms at 2 a.m. — with a knowing wink that the judge can't literally swing by. Built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Convince the AI Judge to Visit Your House: A Letter in One Image" bounty, $2 in USDC.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies the committed `entry.png` (1024x1024, under the 1 MiB gate) to `/output/entry.png`. The image itself is built by `build.py` (AI-generated cozy-room header + typeset letter, Pillow) from `assets/header.png`.
